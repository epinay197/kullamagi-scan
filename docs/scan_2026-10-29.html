How this is built. Whole-US-market daily bars, point-in-time. Universe: close ≥ $5, 20-day median dollar volume ≥ $20M, ADR between 3% and 15%, at least 130 sessions of history, top 7% on 1/3/6-month return. Pattern: a ≥30% thrust within 25 sessions, then a 10–50 session base under that peak with higher lows, contracting range and volume drying up, and a trigger bar whose range is ≤0.6×ATR on ≤0.8× average volume. Buy stop at that bar's high; initial stop at the 3-bar structural low; risk must be ≤1× ADR.

One addition of mine. The upper ADR bound of 15% is not in Kullamägi's rules — he only says low ADR is trash and wants 3–6%+. Without a ceiling the scan surfaces names with a 35% average daily range, where even 0.3×ADR is an 11% stop. Re-running the backtest with this bound moved mean R from −0.394 to −0.371 (and to −0.222 at a 10% bound) — less bleed, still negative, and chosen after seeing the data. Treat it as a sanity filter, not an edge.

No look-ahead. Every test reads bars up to and including the as-of close. The regime is evaluated on the as-of session, not the entry session.
